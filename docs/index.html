ASQ Survey

Airport passenger satisfaction, collected at the gate

A tablet at the departure gate asks passengers how their journey through the airport actually went — 31 service-quality items, five emotions, and two open questions — and emails every completed response out as structured data.

No backend, no database, no API keys. Responses are written to the device first and sent second, so a Wi-Fi drop in the terminal costs nothing.

Where the responses go

Each completed survey becomes one email, carrying the same data three ways so it is usable whether you are reading it or parsing it:

  • a Question → Answer table, readable as an email with no tooling
  • payload_json — the full nested response, for parsing
  • csv_header and csv_row — for pasting straight into a spreadsheet

The recipient address is never in the code. You type it into the app's settings on the device itself, then swap in the forwarding alias the mail relay gives you back.

Reading the results

The companion report tool turns those CSV files back into a report: drop in the exports from every tablet and it merges them, drops duplicates, and produces the same shape as an airport benchmark deck — overall satisfaction and its distribution, the eight category scores, all 31 items ranked, passenger profile, emotions, and month-on-month movement.

It adds the analysis a benchmark report does not give you: what to fix first — the items passengers score below your own average and named among their three most important. Something scoring badly that nobody cares about is not the priority.

Reports are built in your browser. The CSV files are never uploaded, which matters when the free-text answers came from members of the public. Prints cleanly to PDF.

Built for a kiosk

  • Idle reset — 90 seconds without a touch discards the half-finished response, so one passenger's answers can never leak into the next one's
  • Settings behind five taps and a PIN — a passenger cannot reach the recipient
  • Offline queue — responses flush when the network returns; the header shows how many are waiting
  • Wake lock — the screen stays on, and the lock is retaken after backgrounding

Setting it up

  1. Install the APK on the tablet, or just open the browser version and add it to the home screen.
  2. Tap the logo five times, enter the PIN (1982 by default — change it).
  3. Enter your recipient address, the airport code, terminal and gate. Save.
  4. Complete one survey to trigger the mail relay's confirmation email, and click its link.
  5. Paste the alias it replies with back into the recipient field, so the plain address is no longer stored on a device standing in a public hall.
  6. Pin the app with Android's screen pinning and leave it at the gate.

Privacy

The survey records no name, seat or booking reference, and the welcome screen tells passengers so. Responses stay in the tablet's local storage until they are emailed, and for 30 days after; Clear queue in settings deletes them at once. Decide who owns the recipient mailbox, and how long responses live there, before a tablet goes airside.
